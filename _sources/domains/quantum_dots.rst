.. _domains_quantum_dot_control:

Quantum dots are nanoscale semiconductor structures that can confine and control individual electrons, forming artificial charge islands with applications in single-electron electronics, quantum information processing, and sensing. Despite their promise, operating quantum dot devices remains a major experimental challenge. Device tuning has traditionally relied on expert intuition, trial-and-error procedures, and time-intensive measurements, making it as much an experimental “art” as a systematic workflow. These heuristic approaches become increasingly impractical as quantum dot systems grow in size and complexity. Scalable quantum technologies will require automated methods that can reliably characterize, tune, and control devices with reduced human intervention. The task described in this test captures a foundational capability for quantum dot control. As models improve, the test will expand to include increasingly complex tasks, providing a pathway for evaluating progress toward more autonomous quantum dot operation.
