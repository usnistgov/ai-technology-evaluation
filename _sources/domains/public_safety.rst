.. _domains_audio_classification:

When public safety events occur, a swift and effective response is paramount, and failure to respond quickly and appropriately can result in major disruptions, as well as loss of lives and property that could otherwise be avoided. Real-time public safety imagery enables emergency personnel to optimally assess and respond to public safety events. However, the continuous monitoring, especially a large number, of imagery sources is a known challenge for humans. The task described in this test represents a basic ability to identify public safety events from imagery data. As models demonstrate their abilities and improve over time, increasingly complex tasks in support of public safety event recognition, potentially including additional modalities, will be added to the test.
